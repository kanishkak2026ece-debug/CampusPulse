CampusPulse

Your Campus, Your Updates!

CampusPulse is a campus-focused platform designed to help students stay updated about college activities, events, workshops, and technical fests.

Features

- View campus events and activities.
- Discover technical fests.
- Explore AI-related events.
- Stay informed about workshops.
- Access important campus updates.

Purpose

The main goal of CampusPulse is to make campus information easily accessible to students in one place.
